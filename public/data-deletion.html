Data Deletion

You can delete your Bio-One Social Hub account and all associated data (profile, connected-account tokens, drafts, scheduled posts, and post history) at any time.

How to request deletion

  1. Email [privacy@bioone.com] from the email address associated with your account, with the subject "Delete my Bio-One Social Hub data".
  2. We will permanently delete your account and associated data within 30 days and confirm by email.

Disconnecting a single platform

To remove just one connected social account (and its stored access token) without deleting your whole account, log in, go to My Accounts, and click Disconnect next to that platform.
