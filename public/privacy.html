Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Bio-One ("we", "us") collects and uses information in the Bio-One Social Hub application (the "Service"), which lets Bio-One franchisees publish marketing content to their own connected social media accounts.

Information we collect

  • Account information you provide when you sign up: your name, franchise location, and email address.
  • Authentication data for social accounts you choose to connect (e.g., access tokens from Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, Pinterest, or TikTok). We use these only to publish the posts you create.
  • Content you create: captions, schedules, and the posts you publish or queue.
  • Basic usage data needed to operate the Service.

How we use information

  • To let you create, schedule, and publish posts to the accounts you connect.
  • To show you your own activity, history, and analytics.
  • To notify you (by email) if a post fails to publish.
  • To operate, secure, and improve the Service.

How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share content only with the social media platforms you choose to connect, in order to publish on your behalf. We may use service providers (e.g., hosting, email delivery) who process data on our behalf under confidentiality obligations.

Data retention & deletion

We keep your information for as long as your account is active. You can request deletion of your account and associated data at any time — see our Data Deletion page.

Your choices

You can disconnect any social account at any time in the Service, which removes the stored access token for that platform. You can request access to or deletion of your data using the contact below.
