Impression Share & ROAS

Use this to explain why owners on paid should consider scaling the account in busy season to gain more share of the market.

Search Impression Share

Are you even showing up?

Impression share shows how much of the market you're reaching.

It's the percent of searches where your ad actually appeared, out of all the times it could have.

Example: If your impression share is 40%, you're missing 60% of the people looking for help right now.
The searches you miss don't disappear — a competitor shows up instead.
Why it might be low
  • Budget runs out before the day ends, so the ads stop showing.
  • Bids are too low to compete with others going after the same searches.
How to improve it
  • Increase the budget so ads can run all day, especially in busy season.
  • Keep bids competitive for the area.
  • Check that location and targeting cover the full service area.
ROAS · Return on Ad Spend

Is the PPC spend paying off?

ROAS is how much revenue comes back for every dollar spent on ads.

A 5x ROAS means $1 in ads brought back $5 in jobs.
What the number tells you
Strong The account is working well and can likely handle more budget.
Low Usually something needs adjusting — targeting, keywords, or budget. It does not mean ads should be turned off.
Turning ads off in busy season hands the demand straight to competitors.

Why Scale in Busy Season

More demand, more to win.
More searchesWhen demand goes up, more people are searching — but so are your competitors.
Flat budget = lost shareIf budget stays flat while searches climb, your impression share drops.
Dollars work hardestBusy season is when each dollar tends to work hardest — the best time to grow, not hold back.
Capture the marketScaling while ROAS is strong captures more of the market while the demand is actually there.
